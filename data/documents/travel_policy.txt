BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Employees traveling for approved business purposes may request reimbursement for eligible travel expenses.

Business travel must receive manager approval before the trip.

Employees should use company-preferred travel providers when available.

Travel expenses must be supported by receipts when required by company policy.

Employees should submit travel expense reports within 15 business days after returning from a business trip.

Questions about travel reimbursement should be directed to the Finance team.
