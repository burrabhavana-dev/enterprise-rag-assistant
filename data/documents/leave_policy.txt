EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive annual paid leave based on their employment category.

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.

Employees must submit leave requests through the employee portal at least 5 business days before the requested leave date.

Emergency leave may be requested with less notice when unexpected circumstances prevent advance submission.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward for up to 10 days into the following calendar year.

Employees should contact Human Resources for questions about leave eligibility.
