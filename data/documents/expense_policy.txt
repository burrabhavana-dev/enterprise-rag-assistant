EMPLOYEE EXPENSE POLICY

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred while performing authorized company activities.

Expenses must be related to legitimate business purposes.

Employees should retain receipts and supporting documentation for reimbursable purchases.

Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.

Expense reports should include the business purpose, date, amount, and appropriate supporting documentation.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving employee expense reports.
